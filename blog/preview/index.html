Went all-in on Mac about a year ago now, and haven’t looked back since. Love my silent little machine, the display and trackpad are especially great and the software complements them nicely. I even found the stock apps to be sufficient for the majority of everyday tasks.


Enter Preview, the bundled image viewer — and to my surprise — somewhat of an editor as well. I can do most of the rough YTP / sbubby-esque image editing I did in Paint with it. You can easily copy bits of images into others, move, size, and rotate them to fit however you’d like. With all that power, more simple tasks would be a breeze, no? For other simple tasks yes, but...


I had a 16:9 version of a render I wanted to use as a track cover for my site, so I have to crop it down to be a square. With all that knowledge, I think “I shouldn’t have to open an editor to do this.”


I look for an obvious crop option in the interface. Nothing.


Then I check online for a solution.


Tutorial videos suggest I select an area, then hit cmd+k to crop it. Not what I’m looking for. That’s too imprecise, I need an exact aspect ratio. Blogs and videos suggest I try Tools > Adjust size. That scales the image by squishing it, not cropping it. Again, not what I’m looking for. Answers to this exact question on forums seem to mirror this same sentiment. No dice. The best I can think to do in Preview is hold Shift to make my selection box a square, size it to fit the image, then crop. Then I still have to eyeball align that square selection to be centered. For fucks sake.


Yes, yes. I know. This whole time I could’ve just opened an editor and been done with it. Or even without installing an app, I could import the image into Photos, crop it there, then drag it back out, but that’s obtuse. It’s just baffling. Gwenview can do this. Loupe can do this. Even Windows Photos can probably, too, and not to mention every smartphone.


Apple is so good at convenience features and small, nice touches that make their products feel lovely to use, but they sometimes leave gaping holes in the forms of fundamental functionality. In some cases it makes sense, like not being able to make a new file by right clicking an empty space. They expect you to start your file in an app then save it. Yeah whatever. Some just don’t.


I ended up opening Pixelmator and cropping it there. End of story. Sigh.

Be on the lookout for a new track on my birthday (the 8th).
